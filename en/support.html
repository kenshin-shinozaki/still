Support

How still. works, and what to check when something seems wrong.

01What you need

  • An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later
  • Songs in your Music library or an Apple Music subscription (playback goes through the Music app on this iPad)
  • Access to Media & Apple Music

02Questions

No albums appear, or it says access is not allowed

Open the Settings app → still. and turn on Media & Apple Music. If your library is empty, add songs to it in the Music app.

The music does not start the moment the needle lands

After the needle touches the record, a few seconds of groove noise play before the song begins (by design). Changing tracks also lifts and resets the needle with a short pause.

The music stopped and a turn-over key appeared

Albums are split into side A and side B between songs and stop at the end of side A. Press the turn-over key for side B.

Changing the colours

The gear in the top-right corner lets you choose a night or bright room and a Modern or Walnut deck.

Seeing the track list

Tap the sleeve to turn it over and see the track list.

Seeing the guide again

Open the gear in the top-right corner and choose How it works to open the first-run guide at any time.

03Contact

Please send problems and feedback by email. Mentioning your iPad model and iPadOS version helps.
