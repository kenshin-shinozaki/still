PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

still. (“the app”) does not collect personal information.

  • Nothing collected

    No personal information, no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

  • Nothing sent

    The app sends nothing to a server, and the developer receives nothing.

  • Stays on your iPad

    Your library is read only to show and play it. Your colour choice is stored on the iPad.

01Information we collect

The app does not send any information to a server, and the developer does not receive any. There is no account, advertising, analytics or tracking.

02Information used on your device

  • Music library — only if you allow access to Media & Apple Music, the app reads album and playlist names, song titles, artist names, artwork, song lengths and playback state on your iPad to display them and control playback.
  • Settings — your choice of colours for the room and the player is stored on your iPad and is removed when you delete the app.
  • Device motion — used only for lighting and perspective effects on screen; never stored or sent.

03Sharing

The app does not share your information with anyone. Music playback and your Apple Music subscription are governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

04Changing access

You can change the app’s access to Music at any time in the Settings app → still.
